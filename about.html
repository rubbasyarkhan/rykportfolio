About
Me

I design with intention,
not noise.

I'm a digital product designer and developer focused on creating interesting, functional experiences that feel human. My work lives at the intersection of structure and emotion — where clarity meets craft.

Over the years, I've learned that good design isn't about being loud or super complex. It's about removing friction, respecting the user's attention, and letting ideas breathe.

"Good design is as little design as possible."

— Dieter Rams, Industrial Designer

Outside of work

When I’m not designing, I spend my time watching films, talking to people, and observing the details of architecture and everyday life.

I’m especially drawn to movies in the psychological thriller, Mob, and mind-bending genres — stories that play with perspective, tension, and emotion.

These experiences influence how I think about storytelling, mood, and composition. They remind me that meaningful design, like a great film, is intentional, immersive, and timeless.
