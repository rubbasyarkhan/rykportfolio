THE CHALLENGE

The challenge was to create a website that reflects the high-energy and creative nature of Carving Production. The previous design was static and didn't effectively showcase their video portfolio. We needed a solution that felt alive, dynamic, and premium.

THE SOLUTION

I designed a motion-centric interface where video content takes center stage. The new design features large-scale typography, immersive video headers, and a dark, cinematic aesthetic that allows the colorful video work to pop.

Key Features

Immersive Video

Full-screen video backgrounds and hover-activated previews to immediately showcase production quality.

Dynamic Grid

An asymmetric grid layout that breaks the mold of traditional portfolio sites, adding visual interest.

Cinematic Typography

Bold, oversized typography that anchors the design and provides a strong brand identity.
