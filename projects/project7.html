THE CHALLENGE

The main objective was to create an engaging and performant 3D environment directly within the browser. The challenge lay in optimizing 3D models and lighting for smooth real-time rendering without relying on heavy external plugins or sacrificing visual quality.

THE SOLUTION

I utilized Three.js to build a custom 3D scene that users can interact with. By implementing efficient geometry and optimized textures, I ensured a fluid experience. The result is a fun, immersive playground that demonstrates the power of modern web graphics.
